Sirocco is a minimal Hugo theme built for personal sites that want structure without locking content into rigid page templates.

It follows a section-driven model: global behavior lives in `hugo.yaml`, section behavior lives in each section's `_index.md`, and individual articles stay lightweight with page-level front matter.

The goal is to keep the presentation calm and readable while making the theme configurable enough that most changes happen in content and configuration rather than in template code.
